14"""Format-neutral annotated variants and their factory. 

15 

16The factory is a port of the format-independent half of Perl VEP's ``OutputFactory.pm``. A variation 

17first becomes a hash of what is known from its position alone (``variation_hash``). That hash is 

18copied and extended once per (allele, feature) overlap (``allele_hash``). The copies are then 

19filtered, picked or squashed as the options ask (``output_hashes``), and ``build_annotated_variant`` 

20hands them to ``AnnotatedVariant.build``, which detaches the variant's own fields. The snapshot is 

21the only thing that crosses a worker boundary and the only thing an output format sees. 

22 

23Only fields this model can actually supply are ever populated. A column VEP fills from data not 

24carried here yet -- HGVS, SIFT, frequencies, co-located variants -- stays empty rather than being 

25approximated, which is VEP's own convention for "not available". The *column layout* for a given 

26set of flags is VEP's, so a downstream tool reading the ``Format`` declaration finds what it 

27expects. 

28 

29Consequences reach an allele through ``VariationAllele.transcript_variation_alleles``. Assigning 

30that list invalidates the allele's aggregate, so assign each overlap's consequences first and 

31attach the overlaps afterwards. 

32"""
