ensembl.vep.corpus package#

Corpus construction and management.

A corpus contains variants and their (variant, feature) overlaps, however they were obtained. Keeping variants explicit preserves inputs with no feature overlap instead of forcing consumers to reconstruct variants from derived overlap records. The synthetic generator lives here rather than under annotators/ because it depends only on common and models and backs the session-scoped fixtures of the whole test suite, not one subsystem.

ensembl.vep.corpus.annotate module#

A whole corpus in the shape an annotated file has: one variation per variant, its overlaps attached.

A corpus holds variants plus overlaps against their standalone alleles. A run – and anything reading a run’s output, like the summary – works on Variation objects whose alleles carry their overlaps. This rebuilds that shape, including variants with no overlaps, so a corpus can be summarised or written exactly as an annotated file would be.

ensembl.vep.corpus.annotate.annotate_and_attach(annotator, allele, overlaps)[source]#

Annotate an allele’s overlaps in one batch and attach them, consequences first.

The order is load-bearing. VariationAllele.transcript_variation_alleles carries on_setattr=invalidates("_overlap_consequences"), and that aggregate is built lazily from the overlaps’ own consequences. Assigning the consequences first means the aggregate is always computed from complete overlaps; the other way round, anything reading it in between would cache a stale, empty answer.

Parameters:

  • annotator (Annotator[OverlapConsequence]) – The annotator to run.

  • allele (VariationAllele) – The allele the overlaps belong to. An allele with no overlaps is attached an empty list, which is what makes it intergenic downstream.

  • overlaps (Sequence[TranscriptVariationAllele]) – The allele’s overlaps, each already built against allele.

ensembl.vep.corpus.annotate.annotated_variations(corpus, annotator)[source]#

Yield one annotated variation per generated variant, in corpus order.

A variant’s overlaps are contiguous in a corpus – one per feature in its flank window – so grouping by name preserves their order. Variants with no overlaps are still yielded with an empty attached-overlap collection.

Parameters:

  • corpus (Corpus) – The corpus to annotate.

  • annotator (Annotator[OverlapConsequence]) – The annotator to run.

Yields:

Variation – Each variant, its allele’s overlaps rebuilt against it and annotated.

ensembl.vep.corpus.describe module#

Describing what a corpus holds, before any annotator has said anything about it.

Shape only: chromosomes, transcripts, variants by class, how the variants pair into overlaps, and the overlap listing that gives vep explain its --name/--index/--variant selectors. What an annotator makes of the corpus is a run summary, and lives with the stats.
