Ensembl VEP#

Ensembl VEP (Variant Effect Predictor) predicts the functional effects of genomic variants.

NOTE: This project is in an initial development phase. Many features are incomplete, APIs may change without notice, and documentation is partial. Use at your own risk and feel free to open issues or contribute; your feedback is welcome.

Code of Conduct#
